Frequently asked

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Settings → Delete Account. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently removed.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple or Google. On iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. We can't cancel on your behalf because billing runs through the platform.

Why does my valuation seem off?

Sparrow Collect estimates prices using machine-learning models trained on aggregated marketplace data. Estimates are informational, not appraisals. Thin markets (rare items, low recent activity) can show wider price ranges. You can refresh comps on demand from the item detail screen.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. We'll email a reset link. If you don't see it, check spam, then email us at support@sparrowcollect.com.

Is my data exported when I delete my account?

You can export your collection (CSV) from Settings → Export before deleting. After deletion the data is gone and we can't recover it.

How accurate is Sparrow Collect's AI scanning?

Vision-based item identification is best-effort and improves with corrections. If a scan misidentifies an item, tap Correct on the result — your correction trains future scans for everyone.
