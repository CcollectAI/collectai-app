Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CollectAI

CollectAI is a mobile app for iOS and Android that uses AI to scan, identify, and value collectibles. You take a photo of any collectible item, and the app identifies the exact item, edition, and variant, then shows you its real-time market value based on actual sold listings from 37 marketplace sources.

CollectAI uses an AI vision engine to analyze photos taken with your phone camera. The AI identifies the specific item, its edition, variant, and condition. It also supports barcode and ISBN scanning for sealed products, books, and LEGO sets. No manual entry or searching is required.

CollectAI supports 54 collectible categories including trading cards (Pokemon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, sports cards), figures (Funko Pop, Hot Toys, anime figures, Gunpla), media (vinyl records, Blu-ray steelbooks, manga, comic books, retro games), luxury items (watches, fragrances, pens), building sets (LEGO, Warhammer, scale models), and lifestyle collectibles (K-pop, Disney, Studio Ghibli, Loungefly, artisan keycaps).

CollectAI aggregates pricing data from 37 marketplace sources including eBay sold listings, TCGPlayer, StockX, Cardmarket, Discogs, BrickLink, Mercari, Vinted, WhatNot, Catawiki, Mavin.io, Google Shopping, and Etsy. Prices are based on actual sold listings and active market data. Each item receives a low, mid, and high price range calculated by a machine learning model.

Point your phone camera at any Pokemon card and CollectAI identifies the exact card, set, edition, and variant. It then shows you the current market value based on recent sold listings from eBay, TCGPlayer, Cardmarket, and other sources. This works for all Pokemon card types including holos, reverse holos, full arts, and graded cards.

CollectAI is free to download and use. The free tier includes AI scanning, collection tracking, and basic pricing. A Pro subscription unlocks advanced features like portfolio analytics, condition grading tools, set completion tracking, and unlimited price alerts.

CollectAI uses a machine learning model that analyzes actual sold listings from 37 marketplace sources. Each item receives three price points: a low estimate (10th percentile), median estimate (50th percentile), and high estimate (90th percentile), along with a confidence score. The model continuously improves through user feedback and new market data.

CollectAI supports 7 currencies: EUR (Euro), USD (US Dollar), GBP (British Pound), JPY (Japanese Yen), KRW (South Korean Won), AUD (Australian Dollar), and CAD (Canadian Dollar).

CollectAI differs in three key ways: (1) it uses AI vision to identify collectibles from a photo rather than requiring manual search, (2) it aggregates pricing from 37 marketplace sources instead of relying on a single marketplace, and (3) it provides probabilistic price ranges (low, mid, high) based on machine learning rather than a single estimated value. It also covers 54 categories in one app, whereas most alternatives focus on a single category like cards or figures.
